## Layout and symbols

- `–` bullets; indentation is nesting.
- A horizontal rule over the left part of the page, sometimes with tick marks, separates
  topics.
- `⇒` at the end of a line, repeated at the start of the next: one implication wrapped
  across the break.
- `↑` with a short phrase in free space: an annotation on what the arrow points at.
- Cross-hatched squares in the margins: doodles.

## Language

Switches by block, rarely mid-sentence. English terms inside Hungarian take Hungarian
suffixes (switchek, priornak, priorok) and loan words are spelled the Hungarian way
(kontext, kauzalitás, obszervációs, evidencia).
