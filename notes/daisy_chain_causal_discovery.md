# Daisy chain as autogenous active discovery: working note

**Status:** speculative direction, not a result. Written to restart reasoning, not to summarise for an audience. Assumes familiarity with the daisy-chain derivation note (the MB/BI/EC loop, the misspecification signal, the line-316 derivation issue) and the Kelly-betting experiment note (the $G = \log 2 - H - \mathrm{KL}$ readout). Cross-references to both are made inline.

---

## 1. The core idea

The daisy chain alternates three steps: **MB** (Blahut–Arimoto on $\mathcal{L}_{dyn} = I(\theta;\tilde x) + I(\theta; X\mid\tilde x)$, producing a new discrete prior + an *expected* information gain), **BI** (apply the prior to the next $n$ observations, producing a *realised* information gain), and **EC** (update the likelihood). The **misspecification signal** is expected $-$ realised information gain.

The new move under consideration: regard the sequence of representations the MB step emits as a sequence of *tasks*, generated by the agent itself rather than handed to it by the environment. Under that reading the daisy chain is an **autogenous** transfer/learning process — a single agent that manufactures its own task sequence — and the question is whether this sequence, together with the misspecification signal, can be understood as a form of **active causal discovery within one agent**.

Two framings to keep distinct, because they have opposite epistemic characters:

- **Environmental** task series: the task index corresponds to a genuine regime/environment change. This is the setting where the machinery of invariance-based and exchangeable-data causal discovery transfers most directly. *Set aside for now* — interesting but not the target.
- **Autogenous** task series: the chain generates its own next representation of a possibly-fixed latent. **This is the target.** It is closer to active-sensing / "active inference without action" than to experimental-design causal discovery, and its non-triviality rests entirely on the misspecification signal meaning what we need it to mean (see §4).

## 2. What is and isn't being claimed (the normative question)

The MB step has a **per-step normative justification**: under a finite-horizon information objective (Bernardo / Mattingly et al. 2018), the optimal prior is generically discrete and supplies a normatively-grounded coarse-graining. The worry driving this note: by hanging a causal-discovery purpose on MB, do we void that justification?

Resolution reached: there are two versions of the worry, and we are (or can stay) in the benign one.

- **Version 1 (benign — interpretation, not modification).** MB is left *exactly* as it is. We merely *observe* that the representation sequence it produces, plus the misspecification signal, has the control-flow and error-signal structure of an active-discovery process. One mechanism, two readings, both earned. No threat to normativity.
- **Version 2 (real — modification without backing).** To get causal discovery we are forced to *modify* MB (e.g. add an intervention/action output that chooses experiments on the world), and that addition does not inherit the Bernardo justification.

**The discriminating fact:** does maximising the MB objective $I(\theta;\text{data})$ *also* maximise information about causal structure? This depends entirely on **what $\theta$ ranges over**:

- $\theta$ = a coin bias → "bits about $\theta$" = "bits about a rate". No structure, no discovery. (Current toy.)
- $\theta$ = a causally-articulated latent (mechanism parameters *with a direction*) → "bits about $\theta$" = "bits about the causal factorisation". The *same* normative objective is now also maximising information about structure — **not because MB changed, but because what MB is informative *about* was enriched.**

So enriching the latent keeps us in Version 1. The normativity transfers for free *at the per-step level*.

**Residual risk is trajectory-level, not step-level.** Bernardo justifies one MB step. It does *not* justify the *sequence* converging on the true factorisation. The cleanest defensible position:

> Each MB step is normative w.r.t. its (surrogate) information objective; the *loop* is a **descriptively** active-discovery dynamic whose convergence/fixed-point properties are an open question. Do not claim the loop itself is normatively optimal until the fixed-point analysis exists.

This maps onto the "fixed-point / stability of the loop" row already flagged as unanalysed in the derivation note's gaps table.

## 3. The action link: Kelly betting as a meter, not a new goal

There is no action channel in the chain proper. The link to action is the **Kelly-betting readout** (separate experiment note): the agent bets on outcomes, with reward engineered so that performance reads out information about the latent. Key identity (one-shot Bernoulli, even-money):

$$G(\theta, \hat\theta_{\text{pred}}) = \log 2 - H(\theta) - \mathrm{KL}(\theta \,\|\, \hat\theta_{\text{pred}}).$$

Realised log-growth = constant $-$ entropy of truth $-$ belief-truth divergence. Hence **betting regret $= \mathrm{KL}(\theta\|\hat\theta_{\text{pred}})$ is exactly the information the representation leaves on the table.**

Why this matters for the normative worry: the MB objective ($\max$ expected bits about $\theta$, measured *forward*) and the Kelly regret (expected bits *left behind*, measured *backward*) are **the same functional from two directions**, up to the constant and the irreducible $H(\theta)$. The action channel does **not** introduce a competing telos — it *instruments* the normative quantity. We are putting a meter on the purpose MB already had, in a world that happens to contain action. This is the strongest available footing for staying in Version 1.

**Known limitation (load-bearing).** One-shot Bernoulli Kelly reads out only the **posterior mean**. Two representations with the same predictive mean are indistinguishable to the bet. To make *discreteness* (and later, *causal structure*) visible, the bet must depend on higher moments / joint structure — e.g. non-decomposable payoffs over $k$ tosses where the optimal belief is $\mathbb{E}[\theta^k]$, or joint/conditional bets across positions.

## 4. Connections and differences to existing approaches

### 4.1 Do-Finetti (Guo, Zhang, Mohan, Huszár, Schölkopf, NeurIPS 2024) — and the causal-de-Finetti line

- **Shared premise (real, strong):** non-i.i.d. structure across a series is a *resource* for causal discovery, not a nuisance. Going beyond i.i.d. doesn't cost identification power — it grants it. This is exactly the bet the autogenous-discovery idea makes about the task series.
- **Shared object (suggestive):** both have per-mechanism latents organised into a series — their de Finetti $\theta_i, \psi$ (shared across positions, independent across mechanisms) vs our discrete priors / channel.
- **Divergent mechanism (the gap):** their identifiability *requires multiple environments* = independent resamplings of the mechanisms over a fixed variable set. The autogenous single-agent setting has **one stream and an evolving representation of it** — "misspecified views of one environment", not "multiple environments from one exchangeable process". 
- **Therefore:** the *philosophy* of Do-Finetti transfers; the *theorems* (simultaneous structure + effect identification) do **not**, because we lack the multi-environment resampling. A single long exchangeable stream carries *some* of the cross-position dependence (that's what makes it non-i.i.d.) but not the clean multi-environment identifiability. Be explicit about this line.
- **Closest neighbour:** Reizinger et al. 2024 ("Identifiable exchangeable mechanisms for causal structure and representation learning") is about *representation* identifiability rather than just structure, and is probably nearer to this project than Do-Finetti itself.
- **The promising bridge:** can the misspecification signal play the role that *invariance violation* plays in invariant-prediction-style discovery? There, causal parents = the conditional that is *stable* across environments; non-causal conditionals shift. The misspecification signal is by construction a detector of "the conditional I assumed just shifted." The pattern of *which* representational components produce misspecification signal vs stay quiet is a candidate invariance/variance partition — the de-Finetti currency.

### 4.2 Enhanced POET (Wang et al., ICML 2020) — active self-challenge, but no truth condition

- **Structural mapping (clean):** POET's generate–solve–evaluate loop ≈ MB (propose representation) / BI (optimise posterior) / misspecification (evaluate). "Environment" → "representation/task", "agent fitness" → "realised information gain", "interestingness/minimal-criterion filter" → "misspecification signal".
- **The decisive disanalogy:** POET's interestingness measure is **diversity-seeking with no truth condition** — a challenge is good if novel and solvable; nothing is being converged *toward*. It is a *divergence* engine (open-ended skill accumulation). Surprise-maximisation.
- **Consequence:** if we import POET's logic wholesale, the misspecification signal becomes a *novelty bonus* and the loop becomes representational **novelty search**, which is the *opposite* of converging on a stable causal structure. 
- **What makes ours discovery and not novelty search:** a **convergence target** (the true latent / factorisation), which POET lacks. Information gain *about a fixed structure* shrinks and converges; novelty *about whatever comes next* need not. Active causal discovery (Murphy; Tong & Koller; adaptive design) is information-greedy *to resolve* uncertainty; POET expands it. Our MB objective, being $I(\theta;\cdot)$ about a (hopefully fixed) latent, is of the *resolving* kind — provided the latent is fixed and articulated.
- **Borrowed liability:** POET has *no* trajectory-optimality guarantee — it's a heuristic search. Leaning on the analogy too hard imports the "loop is just a heuristic" character, undercutting any claim the *discovery process* is normative. Reinforces the §2 conclusion: claim per-step normativity, treat the loop as descriptive until proven otherwise.

### 4.3 Active causal discovery / experimental design (the thing we are *not* doing, and why)

- Classical active structure learning chooses **interventions on the world** to disambiguate competing structural hypotheses. That requires an action channel that modifies the data-generating process.
- Our chain controls the **coarse-graining of $\Theta$**, not the world. The honest claim is therefore **active discovery in representation space** ("choosing how to look") **not in intervention space** ("choosing what to do"). 
- Crossing into intervention space is exactly the Version-2 modification that would lack normative backing. Resist it unless willing to pay that price.

## 5. Outstanding issues

Ordered roughly by how much they block the central claim.

1. **Trajectory normativity / fixed point (blocks calling the *loop* normative).** Per-step Bernardo ≠ sequence-level optimality. Need at least an informal fixed-point/stability analysis of the loop. Does the representation sequence converge? To what? Does it converge to the direction-correct factorisation when one exists?
2. **The line-316 derivation issue (blocks calling even the MB *step* cleanly normative).** Unresolved in the derivation note: MB optimises a *surrogate*, not the original MI functional, because of the $1/p_{new}$ pathology. **Direct bearing here:** if the misspecification signal is to mean "structural misfit" rather than "estimator variance", we need Resolution 1 (accept the cross-entropy-like surrogate as the intended objective) to hold, so that "realised < expected gain" tracks genuine misfit and not the variance blow-up. The causal-discovery reading gives an *independent reason* to settle line 316.
3. **Misspecification signal: still no single equation.** "Expected − realised bits" is gestured at but not written down (also flagged in the derivation note). Everything in §4's "promising bridge" depends on having this as a concrete, decomposable quantity — ideally one that can be partitioned across representational components (to read off an invariance/variance partition).
4. **What latent to use (blocks any structural claim at all).** A coin bias has no articulation. Need a latent whose posterior *higher moments and joint structure* are what the reward reads out. (See §6 for the proposed answer.)
5. **Single-stream vs multi-environment (caps the strength of any identifiability claim).** We do not get Do-Finetti identification from one stream. Need to state precisely the weaker claim we *can* make and verify it is non-vacuous.
6. **EC update rule still unwritten.** The loop isn't formally closed until the likelihood-update step is an equation. Inherited from the derivation note; not specific to this direction but gates "closing the loop".

## 6. Suggestions to move forward

**Primary recommendation — the paradigmatic example.** Replace the coin bias with the **bivariate ICM / causal-Pólya latent**: $(\theta,\psi)$ with $X\sim\text{Ber}(\theta)$, $Y\sim\text{Ber}(\psi)\oplus X$ for $X\to Y$ (vs $Y\to X$ vs $X\perp Y$). Rationale:

- Reward channel is **unchanged** — still Kelly on outcomes, still $G=\log 2 - H - \mathrm{KL}$ — but the informative bets are now *joint* ($X,Y$ next position) or *conditional/cross-position* ($Y$ given a fixing of $X$), so the readout grades whether MB captured the **mechanism factorisation**, not just marginal rates.
- The two directions induce **different conditional-bet structures** (the Do-Finetti Block-B asymmetry): a Kelly readout on conditional cross-position bets is *direction-sensitive*. Operational target: **does an MB representation that has "discovered" the right factorisation earn more on conditional/cross-position bets than one that hasn't?**
- It makes the active-discovery story **testable, not interpretive**: run the chain, watch whether the misspecification signal preferentially fires on the representational components corresponding to the *un-modeled mechanism direction*, and check whether responding to it (re-coarse-graining) improves the conditional-bet readout. POET has no analog of this test (no truth condition); we do, via $G$.

**Minimal first step before the full urn.** Use the smallest latent with a single structural bit: two Bernoullis, $X\to Y$ vs $X\perp Y$, with a conditional Kelly bet on $Y_2$ given $X_2$ after observing position 1. This:
- keeps the existing $G=\log 2 - H - \mathrm{KL}$ readout almost verbatim;
- has a "direction" for the misspecification signal to be right or wrong about;
- answers the precondition question — *does direction-sensitivity survive in $G$ at all?* — before investing in urn dynamics.

**Immediate analytic task:** work out the **conditional-bet regret decomposition** for this two-variable case explicitly (the way the single-coin case was done), to confirm the cross-variable term survives in $G$. If it doesn't survive, the whole structural reading collapses at the readout and we learn that cheaply.

**In parallel (unblocking, not on the critical path):**
- Pin down the misspecification signal as one equation, with an eye to making it *decomposable across representational components* (issue 5.3 ↔ the §4.1 bridge).
- Settle line 316 via Resolution 1 and check the surrogate preserves discreteness and $n$-dependence in the existing biased-coin toy (issue 5.2).

**Framing discipline to carry forward:**
- Claim *active discovery in representation space*, driven by misspecification as a structural-misfit signal. Do **not** claim intervention-based experimental design (no action channel on the world).
- Claim *per-step* normativity; treat the *loop* as a descriptive dynamic pending fixed-point analysis.
- Borrow Do-Finetti's *philosophy* (non-i.i.d. as resource), not its *theorems* (multi-environment identifiability we don't have).
- Use POET as a structural analogy for the *loop shape*, while explicitly rejecting its no-truth-condition / novelty-search telos.

## 7. One-paragraph restart seed

We are exploring whether the daisy chain's MB step, run as a loop, can be read as an agent actively discovering causal structure *in representation space* (not by intervening on the world), with the misspecification signal as a structural-misfit detector and a Kelly bet as an information-meter (regret $=\mathrm{KL}$(truth‖belief)). The normative story survives *if* we (a) enrich the latent from a coin bias to a direction-bearing bivariate ICM latent so that "information about $\theta$" becomes "information about the causal factorisation", (b) claim normativity only per-step, leaving loop convergence open, and (c) settle the line-316 surrogate so the misspecification signal tracks misfit rather than estimator variance. The next concrete move is the two-variable ($X\to Y$ vs $X\perp Y$) conditional-bet regret decomposition, to check direction-sensitivity survives in $G$ before scaling to the causal-Pólya urn.
